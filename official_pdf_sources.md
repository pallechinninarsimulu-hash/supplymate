# Official PDF study-resource sources

## JNTUH

The official JNTUH syllabus index is https://jntuh.ac.in/syllabus. It links to the university's remaining syllabus and regulations repository at https://studentservices.jntuh.ac.in/oss/syllabus.html?type=syllabus.

A validated direct JNTUH R22 Computer Science and Engineering syllabus PDF is https://jntuh.ac.in/uploads/academics/R22B.Tech.CSECourseStructureSyllabus2.pdf. The PDF contains the R22 course structure, semester subjects, practicals, electives, project stages, course objectives, and course outcomes.

The JNTUH index is the preferred entry point for other branches and future regulation updates because exact titles and codes can change by branch and regulation year.

## SBTET Telangana

The official SBTET Telangana syllabus page is https://www.sbtet.telangana.gov.in/index.html#!/index/ViewSyllabus. The site is interactive and should be treated as the authoritative source for Diploma syllabus selection, even when direct PDF URLs are not visible in static extraction.

## Usage notes

SUPPLYMATE should link to the official index pages and validated direct PDFs from each regulation/branch where available. Because branch, college, and regulation-year details may vary, the interface should label linked resources as official source material and retain an adaptability notice asking students to confirm their college copy.
