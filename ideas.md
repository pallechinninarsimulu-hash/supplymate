# SUPPLYMATE design direction

## Three initial approaches

### Theme Name: Academic Signal
Very Brief Intro: A calm, editorial study workspace where progress feels measurable rather than stressful. Navy structure, teal confidence signals, and warm paper surfaces make revision feel grounded.
Probability: 0.07

### Theme Name: Campus Field Notes
Very Brief Intro: A tactile notebook-inspired interface with annotated margins, hand-drawn cues, and warm study-room energy. The experience should feel personal and practical.
Probability: 0.03

### Theme Name: Quiet Momentum
Very Brief Intro: A restrained modern dashboard with generous whitespace and precise micro-interactions. It treats each completed topic as a small, visible win.
Probability: 0.09

## Chosen approach: Academic Signal

### Design Movement
Editorial academic modernism, borrowing from university prospectuses, lab notebooks, and understated information design.

### Core Principles
1. Make readiness legible: every score, progress bar, and node should answer a student question.
2. Use structure as reassurance: strong alignment, connected trails, and quiet borders create a sense of control.
3. Balance seriousness with warmth: serif display type and paper-toned surfaces keep the product human.
4. Reward progress with color, not noise: teal is earned; amber is a gentle signal to revisit.

### Color Philosophy
Navy #16233D is the dependable structural ink. Teal #1F9D77 marks completed work and forward momentum. Blue #3D6FB4 is reserved for navigation and in-progress actions. Amber #C97A16 warns without scolding. The canvas stays warm and paper-like so the interface feels like a clear study desk, not an exam portal.

### Layout Paradigm
Use an asymmetric editorial shell: wide reading columns for roadmaps, compact side rails for checkpoints, and cards that feel like pinned study notes. Avoid an overly symmetrical centered dashboard.

### Signature Elements
1. Roadmap trail: connected week nodes that fill with teal.
2. Monospace signals: scores, roll numbers, and week labels use a compact mono face.
3. Paper-panel layering: white study surfaces float over a warm canvas with fine rules.

### Interaction Philosophy
Actions should feel deliberate and low-friction. Completion toggles should immediately confirm progress. Mock tests should feel like checkpoints, not high-pressure exams. Every detail page provides a clear return path.

### Animation
Use short, soft entrance reveals for page groups, 160–220ms hover transitions, and subtle card lifts. Avoid flashy movement. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System
Playfair Display for headings and emotional moments; DM Sans for body copy, labels, and controls; DM Mono for numbers, scores, subject codes, and week identifiers.

### Brand Essence
SUPPLYMATE is a structured backlog-clearance companion for diploma and engineering students who need a calmer way to turn pending subjects into weekly action. Personality: grounded, encouraging, precise.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are clear and quietly motivating. CTAs are specific and active. Microcopy explains the next useful action without pretending that studying is effortless.
Example lines: “Your next clear is closer than you think.” / “Keep moving week by week.”

### Wordmark & Logo
A compact circular “S” mark built like a route node: one navy ring with a teal path cut through its center. The wordmark stays uppercase with generous tracking beside the symbol.

### Signature Brand Color
Supply Teal #1F9D77.
